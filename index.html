• Marvel's Wolverine

    Releasing September 15, 2026 — PlayStation 5 exclusive from Insomniac Games. Logan's first solo blockbuster is a brutal, M-rated action adventure that trades web-swinging for berserker rage. The journey runs from the frozen Canadian wilds to the criminal streets of Madripoor. Adamantium claws, destructible enemies and a healing factor drive its unusually savage combat. It is the most violent story Insomniac has ever put on screen.

  • Grand Theft Auto VI

    Releasing November 19, 2026 — PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, from Rockstar Games. The series returns to the neon coastline of Leonida and a reimagined Vice City. For the first time the story follows a dual protagonist pair, Lucia and Jason, in a modern Bonnie and Clyde spiral. Rockstar promises the densest simulated world it has ever built, from swamp towns to beachfront high-rises. It is comfortably the most anticipated release of the decade.

  • Gears of War: E-Day

    Releasing October 6, 2026 — Xbox Series X|S and PC, from The Coalition. A prequel set fourteen years before the original trilogy, on the day the Locust burst from the ground. A younger Marcus Fenix and Dom Santiago fight through a Sera that has not yet learned how to survive. The tone is horror-leaning, claustrophobic and far less armoured than the war you already know. Chainsaw bayonets return, but this time nobody knows what they are fighting.

  • Phantom Blade Zero

    Releasing October 29, 2026 — PS5 and PC, from S-GAME. A "kung-fu punk" action RPG built on razor-fast parries, deflects and stance switching. You play Soul, an assassin poisoned and given a strict countdown to clear his name. Its dark oriental fantasy world blends Ming dynasty architecture with grotesque, ritual-driven horror. Think Sekiro's precision married to the choreography of a wuxia film.

  • The Blood of Dawnwalker

    Releasing September 3, 2026 — PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, from Rebel Wolves. Built by veterans of The Witcher 3, this dark fantasy RPG is set in 14th century Vale Sangue. Coen is half human, half vampire, powerful by night and vulnerable under the sun. The whole campaign runs on a thirty night time limit that quietly reshapes every quest you take. Choose who to save, and accept the ones you cannot.

  • Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4

    Releasing October 23, 2026 — PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2 and PC, from Infinity Ward. Task Force 141 returns for the next chapter of the rebooted Modern Warfare saga. The package pairs a globe-trotting campaign with a rebuilt multiplayer suite and round-based Zombies. Movement, gunplay and map design are being tuned back toward the classic boots-on-the-ground feel. It also marks the franchise's first appearance on Nintendo hardware in over a decade.

  • Forza Horizon 6

    Out now — launched May 19, 2026 on Xbox Series X|S and PC, with PS5 to follow. Playground Games moves the Horizon Festival to Japan, its most requested setting ever. Mountain touge routes, city night runs and coastal expressways sit inside one seamless open world. A deep tuning and customisation scene leans hard into Japanese car culture. Four dynamic seasons still rewrite the map every single week.

  • Nioh 3

    Out now — launched February 6, 2026 on PS5 and PC, from Team Ninja. The soulslike returns with an open-field structure spanning Sengoku era Japan and a haunted Kyoto. You swap freely between Samurai and Ninja styles mid-fight, each with its own stances and skill tree. Yokai hunting, loot chasing and punishing boss duels remain the beating heart of it. It is the largest and most flexible entry the series has attempted.

  • Marathon

    Out now — launched March 5, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, from Bungie. A PvPvE extraction shooter set on the lost colony world of Tau Ceti IV. You play a Runner, a synthetic mercenary dropped in to loot, survive and escape before the raid clock ends. Bungie's gunfeel is wrapped in a bold, graphic, flat-colour art direction unlike anything else on the market. Every run you lose, someone else keeps.
